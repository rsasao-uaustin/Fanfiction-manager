## 📝 Writing & Editor Features

### Text Editor
- ✅ **Full-featured text area** - Large, comfortable writing space with serif font
- ✅ **Auto-save** - Saves your work automatically after 2 seconds of inactivity
- ✅ **Manual save** - Save button for immediate saving
- ✅ **Chapter titles** - Editable chapter titles with auto-update
- ✅ **Multiple chapters** - Organize your story into unlimited chapters
- ✅ **Chapter navigation** - Easy dropdown selection between chapters

### Writing Statistics
- ✅ **Word count** - Real-time word counting
- ✅ **Character count** - Track total characters typed
- ✅ **Paragraph count** - Number of paragraphs in current chapter
- ✅ **Live updates** - Stats update as you type

### Grammar & Style Checking
- ✅ **Advanced grammar checking** - Powered by LanguageTool
- ✅ **Fallback checking** - Basic pattern matching when LanguageTool unavailable
- ✅ **Error highlighting** - Click errors to jump to location in text
- ✅ **Context display** - See surrounding text for each error
- ✅ **Error categorization** - Different types (capitalization, spacing, word choice, etc.)
- ✅ **Common mistake detection**:
  - Capitalization (i → I)
  - Multiple spaces
  - Missing spaces after punctuation
  - their/there/they're confusion
  - your/you're confusion
  - its/it's confusion
  - Excessive punctuation (!!!, ???, ....)
